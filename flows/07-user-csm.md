# User / Client — CSM (Last Step)

> After final approval, the client fills out the Customer Satisfaction Measurement (CSM) form.
> This is what marks the request **Completed**.

## Happy Path

**Step 1 — Open the approved request**
You'll see an **Approved** request with a **CSM Pending** prompt.

> 📸 _Screenshot here_

**Step 2 — Fill up the CSM form**
Open the request and complete the **CSM (satisfaction) form**.

> 📸 _Screenshot here_

**Step 3 — Submit**
Click **Submit**. The status becomes **Completed** and the request lifecycle is done.

> 📸 _Screenshot here_

**Step 4 — Get the signed PDF**
The final PDF (with all signatures) is available to download.
