# Budget Officer — Budget / PPMP Check

> **This stage only exists if a Budget Officer account is set up.**
> If there is no Budget Officer, the request skips this step and goes directly to the Director.

## Happy Path

**Step 1 — Log in and open the Budget Review Queue**
Go to the **Budget Review Queue** to see requests recommended by IDO.

> 📸 _Screenshot here_

**Step 2 — Open the request**
Verify the budget / PPMP allocation for the request.

> 📸 _Screenshot here_

**Step 3 — Approve**
Click **Approve**. Your **signature** is stamped and the request moves to **Director Review**.
